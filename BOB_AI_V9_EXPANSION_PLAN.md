## Decision Reasoning Framework

### Multi-Agent Perspective System

**Agent 1: The Pessimist** (Risk-focused)

- "What could go wrong?"
- Identifies worst-case scenarios
- Flags hidden risks
- Recommends conservative approach

**Agent 2: The Optimist** (Opportunity-focused)

- "Why this could work?"
- Identifies best-case scenarios
- Highlights potential benefits
- Recommends ambitious approach

**Agent 3: The Engineer** (Implementation-focused)

- "How do we actually build this?"
- Assesses feasibility
- Identifies technical constraints
- Recommends pragmatic approach

**Agent 4: The Researcher** (Knowledge-focused)

- "What do experts know?"
- References industry best practices
- Cites research and precedent
- Recommends evidence-based approach

**Agent 5: The Devil's Advocate** (Assumption-challenging)

- "Is our premise wrong?"
- Questions fundamental assumptions
- Suggests alternative approaches
- Recommends paradigm-shift thinking

### Evidence-Based Decision Making

```
Decision Framework:
1. Problem Statement (what are we deciding?)
2. Evidence Collection (for and against each option)
3. Agent Consultation (5 perspectives)
4. Decision Matrix (compare alternatives)
5. Confidence Scoring (likelihood of success)
6. Recommendation (best approach)
7. Documentation (reasoning captured)
```
