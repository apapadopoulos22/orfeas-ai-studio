WHAT IS REPLICATOR?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Replicator is a comprehensive AI-powered system for converting physical
photographs of objects into precise digital 3D models. It combines computer
vision techniques with deep learning to automatically:

  • Detect and calibrate ruler/scale references
  • Extract precise dimensions (mm-level accuracy)
  • Classify object geometry (5 shape types)
  • Identify hidden areas and cavities
  • Suggest optimal photography angles for complete coverage
  • Generate exportable 3D models (OBJ format)
  • Produce detailed analysis reports

WORKFLOW:
  Upload Images → Configure Ruler → Analyze → Review Results → Export
