### Expansion Philosophy

1. **Depth First:** Add specialized sub-disciplines
2. **Breadth Second:** Add new domain categories
3. **Library Integration:** Include 5-10 libraries per discipline
4. **Practical Orientation:** Focus on implementable technologies
5. **Cross-disciplinary Links:** Connect related concepts
